Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ArewaBiz Connect ("we", "us") is an online directory that helps people in Northern Nigeria and beyond find local businesses and contact them directly. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect, how we use it, and the rules for using the site. By creating an account or listing a business, you agree to these terms.

1. Privacy Policy · 2. Terms of Use · 3. Contact us

1. Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Visitors: you can browse the directory without an account. We do not ask visitors for any personal information.
  • Account holders (business owners): your name, your email address and your password. Your password is handled by our authentication service and we cannot see it.
  • Business listings: the business name, category, state, town, address, phone number, WhatsApp number, business email, and description that you choose to submit.

What is shown publicly

Once a listing is approved, its business name, category, location, description, and phone and WhatsApp numbers appear in the public directory so that customers can contact you. Your account email, your account name and your business email are not displayed in the directory. Please do not put information in your listing that you do not want the public to see.

How we use your information

  • to create and secure your account;
  • to review and publish your business listing;
  • to contact you about your listing, for example to confirm that it is genuine;
  • to keep the directory safe and free from fake or harmful listings.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who else handles your information

We use trusted service providers to run the site: a database and login provider (Supabase) and a website hosting provider (Vercel). They process information only to provide these services. Your listing's phone and WhatsApp numbers are also visible to anyone who views the directory.

Cookies and similar storage

We store a small amount of data in your browser to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and listings for as long as you use the service. You can ask us to delete your account and listings at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also edit your own listings after logging in. We handle personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Children

ArewaBiz Connect is meant for adults and registered businesses. Please do not create an account if you are under 18.

2. Terms of Use

Listing a business

  • List only a real business or service that you own or are allowed to represent.
  • Give accurate information, including a working phone number.
  • Every new or edited listing is reviewed by us before it appears publicly. We may approve, reject, edit or remove any listing at our discretion.
  • One business should not be listed many times.

What is not allowed

  • illegal goods or services, stolen goods, or anything that breaks Nigerian law;
  • fake listings, scams, or attempts to collect money or personal details dishonestly;
  • adult content, hate speech, threats, or content that harms others;
  • copying another business's name, photos or contact details without permission.

We may remove listings and suspend accounts that break these rules.

We are a directory only

ArewaBiz Connect helps customers and businesses find each other. We do not sell goods or services, and we are not part of any deal between a customer and a business. We do not guarantee the quality, price, safety or honesty of any business. A "Verified" badge means we confirmed the business's contact details, not that we guarantee its products or services.

Staying safe

  • Check a business and its location before you pay or travel.
  • Be careful about paying money in advance to someone you do not know.
  • Report suspicious listings to us (see Contact us below).

Limits of our responsibility

We try to keep the site accurate and available, but we cannot promise it will always be error-free or uninterrupted. To the extent allowed by law, we are not responsible for losses that result from dealings between users, or from information that users publish in their listings.

Changes

We may update this page as the service grows. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated version.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
